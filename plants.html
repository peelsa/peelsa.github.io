Technical OEM manufacturers

Automate the sales department — without sending the book out of the building.

On-site AI for OEMs with technical products, application engineers who write proposals, and a sales force that sells direct or through a manufacturers’ rep network. Pricing, configs, and customer applications stay on your network.

The job

Proposals stall when the knowledge lives in three heads.

Application engineering rebuilds packages from memory and old PDFs. Direct sellers and independent reps call the factory for the same sizing questions. Account history sits in CRM notes and inboxes no one can search safely in the cloud.

01

Proposal cycle time

Past wins, option lists, and drawings exist — but assembling the next package still takes days.

02

Rep & inside-sales drag

Every “will this work for that duty?” becomes a phone tag with an app eng who is already underwater.

03

Board-level pain

Sales leadership, application engineering, and ownership all feel it — decisions often land with a small board, not a single title.

Application
