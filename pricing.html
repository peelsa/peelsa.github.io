You own the system. Maintenance keeps it current.

Buy-to-own is the default. The recurring fee is system maintenance, one fee for the whole system. A lease is there when you want operating expense instead.

One fee for the whole system, not a charge per person. It covers monitoring, model updates, patches, and the retrieval layer. A small per-seat add-on can sit on top when the roster grows. The tier follows how many systems you run.
