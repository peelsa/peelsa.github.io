Security

Isolation, walls, logs, open storage.

The same controls serve OEM pricing/config IP and firm privilege concerns. Architecture first. Policy second.

Inference

On-prem only

Models run on the leased appliance inside your network. Tokens for your corpus are not sent to a public cloud for inference.

Walls

Payload filters

Retrieval is filtered by product line / region / role — or matter / client / ethical wall — before the model sees a chunk.

Audit

Query log

Every query and every retrieved chunk is logged. That log is the operational and ethics artifact.

Storage

Open & auditable

Plant or firm corpus on TrueNAS SCALE (open ZFS) over fast NFS. Not a closed NAS OS you cannot inspect for phone-home.

Drive layout

Spark internal drive ≠ your files.

On the Spark

  • OS
  • Model weights

On TrueNAS

  • Your corpus and extracts
  • Vector store data
  • ZFS snapshots before updates
  • RAID-Z redundancy
Closed NAS firmware (QTS and similar) is out — you cannot prove there is no backdoor. Open storage is a sales point.
