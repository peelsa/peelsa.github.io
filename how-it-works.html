How it works

The model comes to your data.

Files stay where they are. The system reads them there, and the answer is produced on hardware in the building.

1

Ingest

Read the documents already in the building.

2

Tag

Mark source, and the wall that applies: line and asset, or matter and client.

3

Embed

Build the search index on the local system.

4

Retrieve behind walls

A question only returns passages the signed-in role is allowed to see.

5

Answer with citations

The local model answers from those passages and names the source.

On a plant floor

A technician on Line 3 asks why the filler stopped. Retrieval returns that line's shift report, the vendor manual, and the open work order. Another line's notes are not in the set.

In a firm

A lawyer on the Harbor matter asks for the clause used in that engagement. Retrieval stays inside Harbor. Another client's files are not returned.

Most deployments land in four to eight weeks from first conversation to production.
