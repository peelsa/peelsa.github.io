Law firms and confidential-work practices

Privilege as an architectural guarantee, not a policy promise.

The model answers from matter files that never leave the office. Retrieval is filtered by matter and client. Every query is logged.

What it reads

  • Matter files and work product
  • Brief banks
  • DMS libraries such as iManage, NetDocuments, and Clio, with the same access list
  • Engagement letters and email archives

Each file is hashed and tagged with source, matter, and client before it is indexed. The original stays next to the extract.

Walls and the log

Walls are matter and client. Lawyer A cannot retrieve Client B. Ethical walls are filters at retrieval, not a warning in a handbook.

The log records every query and every retrieved chunk. That log is the record you can show later.
