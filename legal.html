Law firms

Privilege as an architectural guarantee, not a policy promise.

Mid-size firms. Managing partner + ethics counsel + IT. Use AI on your own files the way you use the DMS — walled, logged, never off-site.

The job

AI on matter files — without sending a privileged token off-site.

Lawyers need answers from their own work product and client files. Ethical walls are filters at retrieval, not a handbook paragraph.

01

Matter & client walls

Lawyer A cannot retrieve Client B. Walls are enforced when the system retrieves — not hoped for afterward.

02

Query log

Every query and every retrieved chunk. That is the malpractice / ethics artifact.

03

On-prem by design

The model comes to the files. Privileged material does not leave the building for inference.

Ingest

What ethics counsel will ask about.

Sources

  • Matter files
  • Work product / brief banks
  • DMS (iManage, NetDocuments, Clio) with ACL sync
  • Engagement letters
  • Email archives

Before the index

  • Hash + source + matter / client tags
  • Access labels synced from the DMS where possible
  • Original file kept next to extracted text
  • Ethics counsel review of the wall model is part of onboarding

Same architecture fits other confidential-work practices (accounting / tax, investigations, financial advisory). Not healthcare.
